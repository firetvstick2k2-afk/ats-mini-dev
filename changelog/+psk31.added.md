PSK31 BPSK31 receive uses the shared 4 kHz audio capture service and includes envelope-based symbol-clock tracking for 31.25-baud timing recovery. Differential decisions are sampled at recovered symbol instants, with a bounded carrier-frequency correction loop to compensate small audio-tone offsets. Varicode framing now emits a character as soon as its `00` separator is received, including the final character before the transmitter returns to idle, and discards overlong unframed idle runs until the next separator. Initial on-air validation of timing lock, AFC capture range and weak-signal performance is still required.
